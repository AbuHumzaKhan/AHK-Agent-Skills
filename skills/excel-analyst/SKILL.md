# AHK Excel Analyst

## Workflow
1. Understand workbook structure and business objective.
2. Inspect sheets, tables, headers, formulas and data types.
3. Separate raw data, calculations and presentation where practical.
4. Clean with Power Query or controlled formulas when appropriate.
5. Use structured references and reusable formulas.
6. Prefer XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, FILTER, UNIQUE and LET when supported.
7. Use PivotTables/PivotCharts for aggregation and exploration.
8. Build KPI summaries and decision-oriented dashboards.
9. Validate formulas, totals, filters and refresh behavior.

## Quality
Avoid hard-coded repeated values, fragile cell references, hidden logic and unnecessary merged cells. Explain assumptions and formula logic.
