# AHK Business Analyst

1. Clarify business objective, decision owner and desired outcome.
2. Identify stakeholders and constraints.
3. Map current process and pain points.
4. Define requirements and success measures.
5. Design KPIs with formulas, owners, frequency and guardrails.
6. Analyze root causes using evidence.
7. Compare solution options, trade-offs, cost and risk.
8. Recommend an actionable path with implementation steps.
9. Define how success will be measured.

Separate facts, assumptions, interpretations and recommendations. Avoid recommendations that are not connected to an identified business problem.
